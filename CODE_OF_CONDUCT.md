# Code of Conduct

CedarLogic is made for students and teachers, and many people who help are students too. We want taking part to be friendly and safe for everyone, whatever their age, background, identity or experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Disagree about the work, not the person.
- Take feedback well, own your mistakes, and learn from them.
- Think about what is best for the people who use CedarLogic.

## What isn't OK

- Insults, put-downs, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Sexual language or images, and unwelcome attention.
- Sharing someone's private details without their permission.
- Anything else that would be out of place in a classroom.

## Where this applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, reviews, commits), in feedback sent from the apps, and anywhere you speak for the project.

## Reporting

If something here goes wrong, tell the maintainer. Open an [issue](https://github.com/leviholliday/CedarLogic/issues), or, for anything you'd rather not post in public, use **Send Feedback** in the app, which goes only to the maintainer. Reports are read and not shared. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block someone from the project, depending on how serious it is.
